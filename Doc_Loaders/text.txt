Mia Khalifa is a Lebanese-American media personality, social media influencer, entrepreneur, and former webcam model. Born on February 10, 1993, in Beirut, Lebanon, she moved to the United States during her childhood. She gained widespread online attention in 2014 and later transitioned away from the adult entertainment industry. Since then, she has built a significant presence on social media platforms, where she shares content related to sports, fashion, lifestyle, and current events. Mia Khalifa has also been involved in business ventures, public advocacy, and online commentary, maintaining a large global audience and digital influence.
